## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet grandeur, architectural rigor, and bespoke hospitality. Tailored for an elite clientele seeking exceptional wedding styling, grand stage scenography, and curated culinary experiences, the visual tone avoids decorative clutter in favor of museum-grade composition and editorial restraint.

The aesthetic philosophy draws directly from haute couture editorial print and gallery spatial design:
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Layouts honor structural space, prioritizing extreme breathing room over dense packing. White space is treated as an active framing device rather than empty canvas.
- **Photography-First Storytelling:** Images of floral installations, light-sculpted stages, and gastronomic details dominate full-bleed or precisely proportioned aspect ratios (3:4, 16:9, 1:1), framed by razor-thin guidelines.
- **Tactile Material Honesty:** Eliminates artificial digital artifacts—no diffused gradients, synthetic glassmorphism, or volumetric drop shadows. Physicality is expressed purely through hairline structural borders, warm linen-tinted surfaces, and dramatic typographic hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic neutrality with sharp crimson punctuation. Light mode serves as the primary canvas, echoing fine unbleached cotton paper, while intentional deep obsidian passages anchor high-gravity moments such as testimonials, grand portfolio reveals, and black-tie nocturnal showcases.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#B93328` / `#9E261E` hover):** Malabar Red. Reserved strictly for 5–10% of any view—primary calls-to-action, active stage markers, date highlights, and key editorial monograms.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D96938`):** Warm Terracotta. Used for delicate metadata, seasonal floral tags, hover underlines, and thematic culinary highlights.
- **Base Surfaces:**
  - Background Canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`)
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Panel: Washed Sand (`#F5F2EC`)
  - Contrast Dramatic Canvas: Deep Obsidian (`#121110`) with Container (`#1A1816`)
- **Typography & Boundaries:**
  - Primary Text: Deep Near-Black (`#1A1715`)
  - Secondary / Supporting Text: Muted Warm Charcoal (`#6B6560`)
  - Architectural Dividers & Borders: Hairline Ecru (`#E5E0D8`) on light; Muted Ash (`#2E2A27`) on dark.

## Typography

The typography pairs the dramatic, high-contrast serifs of **Playfair Display** with the sharp, functional clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Playfair Display with standard or slight italic flourishes for key emotional phrases. Maintains generous vertical line height to ensure sweeping letterforms never collide.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-uppercase` handles section markers, category indicators, image captions, and numerical indices. Set strictly in uppercase with extended letter tracking (`0.18em`) to echo luxury brand cataloging.
- **Body & Narrative:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with relaxed leading (`1.6` to `1.7`) to maximize legibility on textured ivory backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column architectural grid inspired by physical print broadsheets and gallery wall arrangements. 

- **Grid Framework:** Maximum container width of `88rem` (1408px) centered with `2.5rem` outer margins on desktop and `1.25rem` on mobile. Columns rely on a `2rem` desktop gutter.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Editorial spreads alternate between wide 8-column hero imagery matched with an offset 4-column descriptive essay, followed by modular 3-column stage breakdowns.
- **Hairline Framing:** Columns and sections are separated using strict `1px solid #E5E0D8` rules, creating a structured canvas that grounds floating imagery.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Major thematic transitions require generous negative space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`). Compact stacking is forbidden; visual pacing forces the viewer to pause and absorb photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved exclusively through **planar stratification, structural contrast, and 1px hairline enclosures**, rather than physical shadow drop-offs.

- **Zero Drop Shadows:** Ambient, blurry, or colored shadows are completely avoided. Drop shadows compromise the authentic, two-dimensional paper-and-canvas quality of the brand.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Layering occurs through color fields:
  - Base layer: Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`)
  - Inset curation cards and hover panels: Washed Sand (`#F5F2EC`)
  - Focus takeovers & night events: Deep Obsidian (`#121110`)
- **Hairline Outlines:** Overlays, menus, modals, and card structures leverage clean `1px solid #E5E0D8` (or `#2E2A27` on dark).
- **Z-Index Layering:** Modals and sticky reservation bars slide across the screen as flat, crisp architectural planes with pure solid backdrops or sharp contrasting borders.

## Shapes

The geometric signature of this design system is **pure right angles (`roundedness: 0`)**.

- **Architectural Edge:** All buttons, cards, image containers, inputs, and flyout menus feature razor-sharp `0px` border radiuses. This reinforces precision, high-end bespoke craftsmanship, and structural solidity.
- **Circular Counterpoint:** The only exception to the orthogonal rule is the occasional circular badge or floral seal stamp (ratio 1:1, `50%` radius), used as an editorial certification mark or floating scroll button.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action:** Sharp rectangular block. Solid `#B93328` background with `#FAF8F5` text, or high-contrast `#1A1715` with `#FAF8F5` text. Padding: `16px 36px`. Typography: `label-uppercase`. Transitions swiftly to darkened tone without translation effects.
- **Secondary / Outline Action:** 1px hairline border (`#1A1715` on ivory, `#FAF8F5` on obsidian). Transparent interior. Text matches border color. Hover shifts background to subtle tonal fill.
- **Editorial Text Links:** Playfair or Plus Jakarta with a permanent hairline underline separated by 4px baseline offset. On hover, the line expands or shifts smoothly to `#D96938`.

### Cards & Curations
- **Portfolio Showcase Cards:** Full-bleed image containers with 0px radius. Borderless by default; delineated by structural grid borders. Captions sit outside the image container below a hairline separator, pairing an uppercase category tag with an italicized event title.
- **Package / Service Cards:** Grounded in `#F5F2EC` surface fill with 1px `#E5E0D8` outline. High internal padding (`40px`). Zero shadows.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Minimal baseline or four-sided crisp box. Neutral `#FAF8F5` fill with 1px border `#E5E0D8`. Active focus switches border to `#1A1715` or `#B93328` with zero outer focus ring glow. Labels use `label-uppercase` floating above the field.
- **Date & Guest Pickers:** Custom calendar grids framed with crisp 1px borders. Selected dates indicated with solid square fill in `#B93328`.

### Navigation & Headers
- **Site Header:** Minimalist, generous vertical clearance (32px padding). Centered bespoke serif wordmark flanked by tracked uppercase navigation items. Sticky state transitions to a condensed bar backed by solid `#FAF8F5` with a single bottom 1px divider.
- **Curated Filtering Tabs:** Segmented row separated by vertical hairline dividers (`|`). Inactive items in `#6B6560`; active item highlighted with an underline or solid `#1A1715` text accompanied by a red dot marker (`#B93328`).
