The Kovalam Coastal Pavilion

Confronted with open ocean gusts and tidal timing constraints, our team engineered a bespoke self-supporting cedar beam canopy that appeared weightless upon the shoreline.

Over forty suspended botanical chandeliers were anchored with concealed ballast weights, retaining full candlelit tranquility without flickering amidst high coastal winds.
