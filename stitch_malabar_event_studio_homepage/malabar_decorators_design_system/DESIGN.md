## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end, editorial, and spacious aesthetic tailored for luxury event production, bespoke stage architecture, and artisanal catering. The visual narrative balances regional warmth and heritage with contemporary architectural restraint. It avoids decorative clutter, faux-luxury metallics, glossy bevels, and artificial glassmorphism in favor of genuine structural elegance, disciplined grid alignment, generous negative space, and evocative photographic immersion.

The target audience comprises discerning couples, corporate gala planners, cultural patrons, and families orchestrating once-in-a-lifetime celebrations who expect uncompromised precision and understated grandeur. The user experience conveys calm authority, curated intimacy, and structural refinement through expansive canvas backgrounds, crisp borders, and rhythmic typography.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in heritage earth tones and refined warmth. The primary color is a rich, heritage Malabar Crimson (`#9B2218` base with `#B93328` used for hover states and subtle emphasis), used sparingly for primary actions, structural rules, and editorial accents. The secondary color is a warm terracota-orange (`#D9531E` base, `#E66734` highlight), reserved strictly for interactive feedback, small contextual badges, and secondary micro-highlights.

Surfaces reject sterile, digital pure white. The canvas defaults to an airy, tactile spectrum: Soft Ivory (`#FFFDFB`) as the base ground, Warm Off-White (`#FAF6F0`) for structural card fills and alternating editorial bands, and Pressed Linen (`#F4ECE1`) for divider rules, hairline frames, and resting input strokes.

Typography and structural lines anchor to Deep Charcoal (`#181513`) for primary content, softened to Muted Umber (`#59514B`) for metadata, captions, and secondary body details. Under no circumstances should saturated multi-color gradients or synthetic neon accents be introduced.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between classical editorial serif grandeur and contemporary geometric legibility.

Headings utilize **Playfair Display**, evoking prestige, literary grace, and bespoke craftsmanship. Headlines maintain generous leading to ensure tall ascenders and delicate serifs breathe cleanly. For category markers, section indicators, and high-level architectural dividers, use uppercase `label-caps` set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** with expanded letter spacing (`0.14em`).

Body copy, functional controls, specifications, data points, and navigation use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clear legibility against pale ivory surfaces. Long-form copy must maintain relaxed line heights (`1.6x` to `1.7x`) to reflect the uncluttered cadence of physical luxury monographs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a structured 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content width of 1440px, flanked by substantial outer margins that create an expansive, gallery-like frame.

- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12 columns, 40px (2.5rem) gutters, 64px (4rem) outer margins. Content density is intentionally airy. Full-bleed photography sections may break the container to span edge-to-edge, immediately followed by centered, column-restricted editorial prose.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px (1.5rem) gutters, 32px (2rem) outer margins. Multi-column showcase cards compress from 3 or 4 columns down to 2 columns with staggered aspect ratios.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (1rem) gutters, 20px (1.25rem) outer margins. Hero displays compress gracefully, full-width edge-to-edge image carousels adopt peek margins (16px reveal of adjacent slides), and vertical section stacking takes priority.

Vertical cadence follows an unhurried rhythm: standard section blocks are separated by `space-4xl` (96px) to `space-5xl` (144px) on desktop, scaling down to `space-2xl` (48px) to `space-3xl` (72px) on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic drop shadows, aggressive multi-layer blur stacks, and frosted glass. Depth is communicated purely through **tactile tonal layering** and **delicate hairline boundaries**.

1. **Ground Plane (Tier 0):** Soft Ivory (`#FFFDFB`). Forms the ambient backdrop of the entire experience.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Tier 1):** Warm Off-White (`#FAF6F0`) bordered by hairline strokes of Pressed Linen (`#F4ECE1`, 1px solid). Used for service cards, catering menu modules, and testimonial panels.
3. **Floating & Sticky Navigation (Tier 2):** Solid `#FFFDFB` with a sharp 1px bottom border (`#F4ECE1`). In scenarios requiring sticky navigation over rich media, a single whisper shadow is permitted: `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(24, 21, 19, 0.04)`.
4. **Interactive Hover Depth:** Hover states on image tiles and curation cards do not elevate via Z-index or shadow expansion. Instead, they respond with micro-scale image adjustments (`scale(1.025)` over 400ms ease-out) and hairline stroke darkening from `#F4ECE1` to `#9B2218`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is defined by an understated softness (`roundedness: 1`). It avoids both razor-sharp clinical industrialism and overtly playful pill geometries.

Interactive controls (buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers) adhere strictly to a crisp 4px border radius (`0.25rem`). Showcase imagery, content cards, and media modules employ an 8px radius (`0.5rem`), softening the boundary between photographic decor captures and the canvas. Circular forms (`50%` / `9999px`) are restricted entirely to functional utility iconography (e.g., carousel arrow anchors, floating audio controls, and index indicators).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Malabar Red (`#9B2218`) fill, `#FFFDFB` text, 4px border radius. Padding: 14px 28px. Font: `label-md` uppercase with `0.08em` tracking. Hover: shifts to `#B93328` with subtle translate-y (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent fill with a 1px solid Deep Charcoal (`#181513`) stroke. Text: `#181513`. Hover: background fills with `#FAF6F0`, stroke shifts to Malabar Red (`#9B2218`), text transitions to `#9B2218`.
- **Text Link Button:** No border or container fill. Deep Charcoal `#181513` text with an offset 1px underline that expands outward from center on hover in `#9B2218`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Text inputs, text areas, and select fields feature an unadorned `#FAF6F0` background framed by a 1px `#F4ECE1` border with a 4px corner radius. Padding: 16px 18px. Placeholder text is set in Muted Umber (`#59514B`) at 45% opacity. Focus state replaces the border with a crisp 1px `#9B2218` outline with zero exterior glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom 18px squares (checkbox) and circles (radio) with 1px `#D9531E` or `#59514B` stroke. Checked state transitions background to `#9B2218` containing a crisp ivory checkmark or centered ivory dot.

### Cards & Media Displays
- **Stage & Decor Portfolio Card:** Image-dominant card with a fixed 4:5 or 16:11 aspect ratio. Container uses 8px radius with overflow hidden. Beneath the photograph, an airy content module displays the celebration venue and event type in `label-caps` (`#D9531E`), followed by the event title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display (`#181513`).
- **Catering & Menu Platter Card:** Elevated `#FAF6F0` surface with a 1px `#F4ECE1` perimeter. Uses asymmetric 2-column or list rows with fine dotted leaders connecting dish titles to culinary provenance notes.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact tags for aesthetic themes (e.g., "Mughal Floral", "Minimalist Mandap", "Bespoke Banqueting"). 4px radius, 6px 12px padding, set in `label-sm` with a pale ivory fill (`#FAF6F0`) and 1px `#F4ECE1` border. Active filter state applies `#9B2218` text with a `#F4ECE1` background.

### Editorial Dividers & Quotation Blocks
- Section separations occasionally feature a centered 48px hairline rule in `#9B2218` or an architectural diamond glyph (`◇`) flanked by extended `#F4ECE1` lines, maintaining heritage nuance without visual clutter.
