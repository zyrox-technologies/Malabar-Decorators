DISCIPLINE 02 / ARCHITECTURAL PRESENCE

Stage & Scenography Decoration

We abandon disposable plywood arches in pursuit of sculptural depth. Our stages employ classical proportioning, curved plaster archways, floating botanical pedestals, and theatrical ambient warm lighting to ensure the focal couple sits in calm, effortless majesty.

  • architecture Curved Archways & Mirrored Portals: Architectural depth that captures ambient room light and frames portraits cleanly.
  • light Warm Chandeliers & Hurricane Ensembles: Hundreds of low-voltage warm points creating multidimensional luster.
  • yard Stone Urns & Cascading Flora: Antique European stone urns laden with blush roses, hydrangeas, and eucalyptus cascades.
